What we do

Two skill sets.
One clean finish.

For builders, homeowners and businesses that want repair work to disappear and fabricated work to look like it was always meant to be there.

01 / RESTORE

Cosmetic repairs

Small defects can dominate a finished space. We repair surface damage neatly and with close attention to colour, texture, profile and sheen.

  • Benchtop & surface chip repair
  • Window sill & frame repairs
  • Wall, plaster & timber patching
  • Doors, cabinetry & fixtures
  • Touch-up painting & finishing
  • Builder defect rectification
02 / FABRICATE

Metal fabrication & welding

Custom steel, stainless and aluminium work sized to the job — from one-off repairs and brackets to rails, frames and fabricated details.

  • Custom gates, rails & balustrades
  • Brackets, frames & fittings
  • Structural & repair welding
  • Stainless fabrication & finishing
  • Polishing & refinishing
  • On-site & mobile welding
